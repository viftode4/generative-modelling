Injectivity and the mixing function




We assume the mixing g : ℝⁿ → ℝᵈ is
injective, often diffeomorphic:

 Injective → distinct latents never collapse to the same x; g is
 invertible on its image.

 Diffeomorphic → smooth map with a smooth inverse.

3 · IDENTIFIABILITY

Identifiable — up to what?




We never recover factors exactly , only up to
tolerable ambiguities. Two milestones:

  Affine equivalence — up to an invertible linear map.
  Too weak: still entangled.

  Permutation equivalence — reordering + per-axis rescaling.
  This is disentanglement.

6 · WORLD MODELS

Causal-JEPA — world models by
object-level masking



JEPA predicts world states in representation space , never pixels.
C-JEPA runs it over object-centric latents and enforces causal
learning by masking object-level latents as a latent intervention.       object-level masked prediction



  Each masked object must be inferred from the others →
  counterfactual-like prediction queries.

  This blocks shortcut solutions (trivial interpolation) and makes
  interaction reasoning necessary.

Assumes an object-centric encoder; actions enter as optional auxiliary
variables.
