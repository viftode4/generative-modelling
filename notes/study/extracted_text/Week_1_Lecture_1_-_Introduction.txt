Outline
Goal: What is Generative AI? Guiding intuitions?

By the end of this lecture, you will be able to
• Outline a brief history of modern AI and trace the development of several
  techniques that are relevant currently
• Identify the goals of AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Generative Modeling
• Discuss Frequentist and Bayesian interpretations of probability
• Contrast discriminative and generative modeling approaches
• Identify the core ideas underpinning different families of generative
  modeling techniques

Project
Goals:
• Hands-on experience with a generative modeling technique
• Deeper understanding of the process going from math to code
• Real-world understanding of engineering challenges

Process:
• Work in groups of 5/6 (split tasks ranging from data processing and
  loading, model implementation, and evaluation)
• Discuss plan with assigned TA
• Share findings in two presentations (mid-term and final)
