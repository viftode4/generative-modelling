We perform iterative optimization!
The Expectation Maximization (EM) algorithm is a powerful technique for finding maximum
likelihood solutions for latent variable models

E Step
• Solve the inference problem: which Gaussian generated each datapoint? or more
  precisely, how responsible is each Gaussian for generating each datapoint?
• Compute the posterior probabilities,


M Step
• Use the assigned responsibilities (the posterior probabilities from the E step) to re-
  estimate the means, covariances, and mixing coefficients
