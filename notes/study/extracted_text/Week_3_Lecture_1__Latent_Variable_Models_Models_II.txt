The Variational Autoencoder (VAE)
A generative latent variable model consisting of 3 key ideas:
1. Use of the ELBO to approximate the likelihood



2. Amortized inference in which an encoder approximates the posterior
   distributions over latents

3. Training is made tractable using
   the reparameterization trick for
   computing gradients
