Project: Implement and experiment with
state-of-the-art generative modeling
techniques
This outcome of the project assignment is an individual report (30% of the assessment).
You will work in groups of 5 students on a specific topic and paper proposed by a TA. The
goal is to implement, experiment with and/or extend different parts of the paper.

Making teams: Use Brightspace to register for projects. Projects are arranged by paper
names. Each paper has been proposed by a TA. The TA is not responsible for the
implementation of your projects but is available to provide overarching guidance.

Formative feedback: You will have 1x feedback at the mid-term presentation and 1x end-
term presentation. These presentations are moments that you can receive feedback as a
group and as individuals in the group. The first presentation is focused on feedback for
project planning and the second presentation is focused on feedback for project
implementation. We aim to inform you on improving your final individual report through
this feedback.

These presentations are not graded and attendance is not mandatory, but they count
towards the “Motivation and Work Plan” part of the rubric of the individual report.

Timeline: After week 4, you should start working on the project along with your group.
Within weeks 1-4 focus on:

   -   Select the paper
   -   Understand the paper and explore the existing code base if applicable,
   -   Prepare an implementation and experimentation plan with your TA, along with a
       division of tasks where you specify who does what. Implementing a technique
       requires multiple components including the data, training, and evaluation pipeline
       in addition to the method itself.

At the end of the course, you should submit an individual report about your project.

Reproducibility project report guidelines

   •   30% motivation and plan: Plan prepared and discussed with TAs, participation in
       group activities, and attend the presentation session.
   •   40% content: show good efforts on trying to reproduce some aspects of the paper
   •   30% writing: critically appraise the paper and explain the experimentation design
       (for example, what was not clear, which results were key to reproduce), state the
       findings from your experiments.

Some projects have existing code implementation. You have the choice to reference the
existing codebase.

Report format: 3 pages double-column (see template). Include the following content:

   •   Introduction (e.g., motivation to implement the paper, what you didn’t like about
       them as a group and as an individual member)
   •   Your approach, experiment setting, implementation details, and results
   •   Your interpretation of the results
